## Notes and limits

- Strategies are simple rules, not real players, so treat these numbers as a guide to relative difficulty.
- The simulation ignores the narrator's lies (only the display changes, never the effects) and playtesters' reading of the text.
- Generated by `tools/balance_sim.cpp`; rerun it after editing `data/events.txt`.

## What changed because of this report

- **First version (no toll on sanity or the wagon):** random play survived 99.9% of runs and even the "worst total effect" strategy survived 100%, so choices barely mattered. The narrator's lies (sanity under 60) appeared in only about 10% of the time played.
- **Tuning:** added a small toll after every part of the day (Food -3, Sanity -4, Wagon -2) and re-ran. A sanity toll of 3 left random play too safe (about 90% survival); 5 was too harsh (about 39%); 4 gave about 69%.
- **Result:** Day 1 is safe (0% of random runs die), deaths cluster in the Day 3 finale, careful play always survives and never reaches Breakdown, and a random player spends roughly half the game with the narrator lying.
